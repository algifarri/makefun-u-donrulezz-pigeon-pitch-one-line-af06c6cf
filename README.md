A deterministic, stdlib-only Python program that writes one one-line elevator pitch (158 characters, English) to `/output/entry.txt`, for make.fun's "Pigeon Pitch, One Line" bounty. A city pigeon pitches investors for its startup with a three-beat escalation — lunch as revenue, statue as office, car as restroom — and closes by raising a round to scale the flock.

## The pitch

> My startup is already profitable: your lunch is our revenue, your statue is our office, and your car is our restroom — I'm raising a round to scale the flock.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
